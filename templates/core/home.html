How an order works

  1. Farmer lists produce. Three quick checks set the grade, and the grade sets a fair price band against today's mandi rate.
  2. Buyer picks a truck. The cost per kg updates with your kilograms added.
  3. The truck dispatches. The delivery cost per kg is locked for everyone on it.
  4. Every receipt shows the split. Farmer share, fee and freight, next to the usual chain.
